Highest Asymmetry
01

The Big Three Mastery Workshop Is Single-Touch Live · No Evergreen Replay

May 13 is one shot. Cold traffic that lands on webinar.saintsofflow.com on May 14 hits a dead funnel until the next live event. Coaches at your stage typically pull 30 to 50 percent of total program revenue from an evergreen replay layer (recorded once, 5-day countdown email sequence behind it, runs 24/7). The webinar you're already filming on May 13 IS the recording. Slotting it into an automated replay funnel and routing applications back into The Flow Code is the most asymmetric play in the funnel because the content cost is already paid.

Cost of Waiting

~$7-17K/mo in recovered revenue from cold traffic that arrives between live sessions, going to no one. That compounds to ~$80-200K/yr while the audience between live dates decays. Every paid traffic dollar that runs to a one-shot live event burns a campaign window too narrow to be profitable.

6 months from now ·~$40-100K never collected. Plus the next quarterly live event burns through the same one-shot pattern, with the same dead funnel between launches.

Fix · Record May 13. Build evergreen replay landing page with 5-day countdown. 5-touch email sequence routing to The Flow Code application. Year-1 floor $80-200K direct + warm-list compounding.
Brand-Completing Tier
02

No High-Ticket Inner Circle Above The Flow Code

The top 5 to 8 percent of Flow Code graduates finish the 13 weeks with nowhere to go. They've integrated the methodology and want a longer-term room with you. Right now there isn't one. A $20,000 to $30,000 12-month room - quarterly retreats, monthly group, quarterly 1:1 - converts the strongest graduates into recurring high-ticket revenue. Most coaches at your stage have an upper-tier room within 18 months of launching their signature program. The Flow Code is past that mark.

Cost of Waiting

~$12-21K/mo in recurring high-ticket revenue never collected - ~$150-250K/yr for the first cohort, recurring at renewal. Each cohort that graduates without an upper-tier room is a class of buyers who would have stayed for year two and didn't because the room wasn't there.

6 months from now ·~$75-125K never collected from the first Inner Circle cohort. The strongest graduates go quiet because there's no room to hold them.

Fix · Inner Circle, graduate-only, $20-30K/yr, capped at 12 to 20 members. Two retreats, monthly group, quarterly 1:1. Year-1 floor $150-250K at 6 to 10 buyers. Compounds with every Flow Code cohort.
Recovery Layer
03

No Tripwire Or Standalone Self Phase Between Webinar And Program

Webinar attendees who can't afford the full 13-week Flow Code (or aren't ready) currently exit the funnel entirely. Phase 1 of The Flow Code (Self phase, weeks 1 to 5: shame work, parts work, somatic work, voice activation) is already built. Slicing it into a $97 to $197 standalone product captures the 5 to 15 percent of webinar attendees who say "I love this but the program isn't right for me yet." The content already exists; it's a packaging question, not a production question.

Cost of Waiting

~$2.5-7K/mo in pure recovery revenue from non-applicants who currently exit cold - ~$30-80K/yr compounding for life of the warm list it creates. Self Phase graduates re-enter the funnel as Flow Code applicants 6 to 12 months later, so the tripwire feeds the program.

6 months from now ·~$15-40K never collected. Plus 200 to 400 webinar attendees permanently lost to the funnel because there was no smaller-ticket on-ramp.

Fix · Self Phase standalone, $97-$197, built from existing Flow Code weeks 1 to 5 video. Year-1 floor $30-80K direct + warm-list re-targeting compounding into Flow Code fill.
Recurring Engagement · Distribution Compound
04

Audience-Yes, Distribution-No · X / LinkedIn / YouTube Long-Form Are Cold

102K IG @saintsofflow + 11.5K Threads prove the brand resonates. But X is essentially absent under the @saintsofflow operator handle, LinkedIn has the profile and the "I help leaders reach their full potential" headline but no content engine for the leader-positioning angle, and YouTube long-form is underweight for a methodology coach at your tier. Your stated ICP is LEADERS / EXECUTIVES - and LinkedIn is the single highest-intent channel where leaders find leadership coaches. You have the Columbia background, the Silicon Valley operator story, the 8-fig acquisition founding-member proof, and 102K IG validating the methodology. What's missing is the content engine that takes a CMO scrolling LinkedIn at lunch and routes them into The Flow Code application.

Cost of Waiting · Per Channel

X (grown to 50K-80K in 12 months): ~$20-50K/yr in Flow Code enrollments never collected. LinkedIn (grown to 25K-50K in 12 months): 4-8 Inner Circle clients at $25K each = ~$100-400K/yr never collected from your stated ICP - this is the largest single-channel lift in the whole package. YouTube long-form (grown to 30K-60K subs): ~$30-80K/yr in Flow Code enrollments plus the asymmetric "home base" value that feeds the evergreen replay funnel (Bottleneck #1).

12 months from now ·$80-300K+ in compounding distribution revenue never collected. Every month the engagement is paused is a month of audience growth and demand-capture that doesn't happen.

Fix · Monthly ghostwriting engagement. X 3x/day, LinkedIn 4-5x/week (leader-positioning cadence), YouTube long-form 1-2x/week (scripts + thumbnail strategy, you or your team films). All written in your voice from existing Flow Code IP - 13-week curriculum, Big Three Mastery transcript, IG reel archive, the Self / Love / Lead methodology footprint. Year-1 floor $80-300K+, on top of the Install bottlenecks. Combined Install + Recurring Engagement Year-1 floor: $340K-$830K+.
