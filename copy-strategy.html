Foundation

Copy Strategy - Jason Crouse

Foundation doc · Voice, brand pillars, headline bank, framing AG1 will write to.

Voice

Grounded, methodical, somatic. 15 years of personal-development experience shows up as restraint, not performance. Inner-work-first language. Secure-attachment vocabulary throughout. Anti-hype, anti-influencer, anti-bro-coach. Jason's voice respects the reader as someone already doing the work, not a beginner who needs to be sold the dream.

Use: flow, secure, somatic, parts, voice activation, vision actualization, thought medicine, purpose, integration, embodied, grounded, the work, methodology, practice, inner, outer, the arc Avoid: hustle, grind, alpha, bro, level up, lifestyle-coach hype, generic "manifest abundance" framing, secret, hack, blueprint (overused), formula (overused)

Brand Pillars

  1. The Big Three - career, love, confidence as the three lanes the methodology touches. Not separate problems. One arc.
  2. Inner work first, outer change second - the Self phase precedes the Lead phase. The voice reflects that order.
  3. Methodology over performance - Jason's credibility is in the methodology, not the lifestyle. The copy should always sound like a practitioner, not a guru.
  4. Earned proof - 12+ named graduates with real outcomes (multimillion-dollar businesses, books, albums, partnerships). The proof is named and specific.

Headline Bank

For the new evergreen Big Three Mastery replay landing, the Inner Circle landing, the Self Phase tripwire, and ad creative:

Evergreen replay (Big Three Mastery)

  • The Big Three Mastery Workshop. On demand, on your time.
  • Career, love, confidence in 90 days. The framework, recorded.
  • The workshop you missed. Watch on demand.
  • The arc from inner work to outer change. In one session.

Inner Circle

  • The room above The Flow Code.
  • For graduates who finished and want more.
  • A 12-month room. Twelve seats. One arc.
  • What comes after Lead Phase. Built for the people who lived it.

Self Phase tripwire

  • The first phase of The Flow Code. Standalone. Self-paced.
  • Five weeks of inner work. Before you decide on the full program.
  • Self Phase, on your own time.
  • The work that comes before the work.

Framing for the Funnel

  • Top of funnel (replay opt-in, ads, IG hooks): inner-work-first hooks tied to specific pain (anxious-avoidant dynamics, voice silence, vision drift, post-acquisition emptiness). No lifestyle hooks. No "transform" framing.
  • Mid funnel (replay landing, Self Phase tripwire): Big Three as the bridge. The webinar gave them the framework; Self Phase gives them the practice; Flow Code gives them the full arc.
  • High funnel (Inner Circle landing): graduate-only framing. The room is built for people who already know the methodology and want the long arc.

Banned Phrases

Universal AG1 ban list, plus Jason-specific: - No "blueprint" or "formula" (overused in personal-development niche) - No "secrets" or "hacks" - No "unlock" - No "transform" (unless paired with concrete graduate-outcome metric) - No "manifest abundance" / "manifest your dream life" - No "level up" / "next chapter" cliches - No first-month-lift language without a stage-specific caveat (this is universal across AG1 copy)

Tone Calibration By Surface

Surface Register Length
IG hook Specific to a phase pain 1 line
Ad copy Methodology-first claim + qualifier 2-3 lines
Email subject Question or somatic prompt, never clickbait 5-9 words
Replay opt-in headline Big Three promise + on-demand qualifier 1 line
Inner Circle headline Graduate-only frame + room metaphor 1 line
Founder section First-person, restrained credentials 4-6 sentences
FAQ Plain answers, methodology-grounded 2-3 sentences each

Voice Test

Every paragraph passes if a Flow Code graduate reading it nods, not laughs. If it could appear in a generic "manifest your dream life" course, rewrite it. The methodology must show up in every paragraph - not as jargon, but as specificity.
