Email Sequences - Jason Crouse

Phase 1 + 2 deliverable · Big Three Mastery evergreen replay sequence (8 touches over 5-day countdown), pre-call (3 touches), post-call (4 touches).

Sequence 1 · Big Three Mastery Evergreen Replay (8 touches over 5 days)

Triggered when a new subscriber opts in for the recorded Big Three Mastery Workshop. The 5-day countdown ends with the Flow Code application close window.

T+0 · Immediate (Replay Access)

Subject: Your Big Three Mastery replay is ready (5 days to apply) Body: Replay link delivered. Quick framing: career, love, confidence are one arc, not three problems. The replay walks the framework. The 5-day window is for graduates of the workshop who want the full 90-day arc inside The Flow Code. CTA: watch the replay first, then read tomorrow's email. Goal: deliver promise, set the 5-day frame.

T+1 day · The Methodology Behind the Big Three

Subject: The order matters - Self before Love before Lead Body: Why most coaches teach mindset and miss meaning. Why Self phase has to come before Love phase. Why Lead phase only works if Self and Love are integrated. One graduate story (named, real - pull from saintsofflow.com testimonials). CTA: replay link if not watched yet. Goal: prove methodology depth beyond the workshop framing.

T+2 days · Why I Built The Flow Code This Way

Subject: What 15 years of personal development taught me Body: Origin story. Homeless to Ivy League to Silicon Valley to founding-team member on the acquisition. The methodology is what got Jason here. The Flow Code is that methodology, packaged for graduates who want the full arc. CTA: Flow Code application page. Goal: founder credibility, route to application.

T+3 days · The Three Things Most People Get Wrong

Subject: Three traps in the inner-work-to-outer-change arc Body: Trap 1 - skipping Self phase. Trap 2 - jumping to Lead before Love. Trap 3 - mistaking insight for integration. Each trap gets a specific somatic or relational marker so the reader can self-diagnose. CTA: application page. Goal: raise the bar on the reader's standard, increase application quality.

T+4 days · Graduate Outcomes (Specific, Named)

Subject: What Brittany did between week 4 and week 12 Body: Pull one specific named graduate story from the saintsofflow.com testimonials. Walk the timeline of their arc inside the program. Specific outcome (business built, book written, partnership rebuilt, album released - whichever fits). CTA: application page. Goal: named proof, route to application.

T+5 days · Last Day (segmented)

  • Application started but not submitted: "Quick - your Flow Code application is half-done. The cohort closes tonight."
  • Application not started: "Last day. Here's what closing the application window actually means - and what the alternative looks like." Soft route to Self Phase tripwire.

T+6 days · Tripwire Routing (non-applicants)

Subject: If The Flow Code isn't right for you yet Body: Honest framing. The Flow Code is the full 90-day arc. If you're not ready for that yet, Self Phase is the first 5 weeks - on its own, self-paced. CTA: Self Phase landing page. Goal: capture non-applicants who would buy a $97-$197 tripwire.

T+10 days · Re-Engagement (warm list)

Subject: What I'm working on between now and the next live workshop Body: Brief teaching note on whatever Jason is currently writing about (parts work, secure attachment, voice activation - rotation). No pitch. Soft re-introduction to the brand. Long-term nurture asset. Goal: keep warm list warm, hand off to broadcast cadence.

Sequence 2 · Pre-Call Nurture (Inner Circle interest, 3 touches before discovery call)

Triggered when a Flow Code graduate submits the Inner Circle interest form and books a discovery call.

T-7 days

Subject: Before our call - 3 questions I'll ask you Body: The three questions Jason will ask, in advance. (1) What changed for you between week 1 and week 13 of Flow Code? (2) What are you holding off naming as your next 12-month vision? (3) What kind of room would actually hold you accountable to it? Goal: prepare the graduate for the depth of the call.

T-3 days

Subject: What Inner Circle actually is (and isn't) Body: Clear framing. Not a mastermind, not a course, not a business accelerator. A 12-month room for graduates who finished Lead phase and want to actualise the vision they named in week 11. Two retreats, monthly group, quarterly 1:1. Capped at 12-20. Goal: set expectations, filter out misalignment.

T-1 day

Subject: Tomorrow at [time] - link inside, 1 thing to bring Body: Calendar reminder, one specific prep ask: bring the vision statement they wrote in week 11 of Flow Code (or the closest thing to it). Read it before the call. Goal: make the call land deeper.

Sequence 3 · Post-Call Follow-Up (4 touches over 14 days)

Triggered after the Inner Circle discovery call regardless of outcome.

T+1 hour · Recap + Next Step

Subject: Recap from our call + the path forward Body: 1-paragraph recap of what was named on the call. Specific next step (Inner Circle invitation if green-light, waitlist if cohort full, alternative path if misalignment). Link to the relevant action. Goal: capture decision energy while it's fresh.

T+3 days · The Specific Resource

Subject: That practice I mentioned on the call Body: Whatever specific resource Jason promised on the call (a parts-work prompt, a journaling sequence, a podcast episode, a retreat venue link). Sent in clean format. Personal touch. Goal: demonstrate depth beyond the sales conversation.

T+7 days · The Check-In

Subject: Quick - did you sit with the vision question? Body: Soft accountability nudge tied to the prep work from the pre-call sequence. "Reply with one sentence on what came up. Or one question." Goal: keep the conversation alive, surface objection or readiness.

T+14 days · The Decision

Subject: Where you landed Body: Clean ask. "Are we moving forward with Inner Circle this cohort or holding for the next one?" No pressure. Clear decision frame. Either route triggers the right next sequence (onboarding or 6-month re-engagement). Goal: decision-time clarity.

Subject Line Bank (rotation)

For ongoing broadcasts beyond the replay sequence:

  • "What I'm reading right now (and why it matters for Lead phase)"
  • "The somatic check-in I run before any hard conversation"
  • "Three questions I'm holding for next quarter's workshop"
  • "What 'secure attachment' looks like in week 12"
  • "The acquisition that changed how I think about purpose"
  • "Why I record one workshop per quarter and replay it the rest of the year"
  • "What graduates do in year two"

Triggers + Tags

  • Tag by replay opt-in date (auto-removes after countdown ends)
  • Tag by Flow Code application status (started / submitted / accepted / declined)
  • Tag by Self Phase purchase ($97 or $197 tier)
  • Tag by Flow Code graduation date (triggers Inner Circle interest sequence at month 3 post-graduation)
  • Tag by content engagement (opened 5+ in 30 days = "warm Big Three reader")
  • Auto-suppress promotional during live webinar week (replace with workshop-specific track)
  • Re-engagement campaign at 60 days of inactivity
