Four Bottlenecks I See for The Flow Code Funnel - Jason Crouse

Three Install bottlenecks ship in less than 7 days from kickoff. One distribution bottleneck layers on top as an ongoing monthly ghostwriting engagement. Built to compound after May 13. Order by impact.

Snapshot

  • IG @saintsofflow · 102K followers, 516 posts (active) + Threads @saintsofflow · 11.5K followers (active) + LinkedIn profile (profile-only, no content engine for the leader-positioning angle) + YouTube channel (underweight at his tier) + X (effectively absent)
  • The Big Three Mastery Workshop · live one-shot webinar Wednesday May 13, 5pm PST
  • The Flow Code 13-week signature program (Self / Love / Lead) · application-gated, group coaching + community bundled
  • 12+ named testimonials live on saintsofflow.com (Brittany Geier, Will Peshoff, Cailin Callahan, others)
  • Jason's lived methodology: 15+ years, homeless to Ivy League to Silicon Valley to high 8-figure acquisition founding member
  • Install gaps: No evergreen replay funnel · No high-ticket Inner Circle above the program · No tripwire / standalone Self Phase below it
  • Recurring engagement gap: audience-yes (102K IG, 11.5K Threads), distribution-no (X / LinkedIn / YouTube long-form)

Bottleneck #1 - The Big Three Mastery Workshop is single-touch live, no evergreen replay funnel (INSTALL)

The asymmetry: May 13 is one shot. Cold traffic that lands on webinar.saintsofflow.com on May 14 hits a dead funnel. Most personal-development coaches at Jason's stage pull 30 to 50 percent of total program revenue from an evergreen replay layer: record once, put a 5-day countdown email sequence behind it, run it 24/7. The webinar Jason is already filming on May 13 IS the asset. Recording it well, slotting it into an automated replay funnel, and routing cold traffic into a 5-day countdown application window is the most asymmetric play in the funnel because the content cost is already paid.

A recorded version of The Big Three Mastery Workshop, hosted on a private replay page, gated behind an opt-in, with a 5-day countdown and a 5-touch email sequence ending in a Flow Code application invitation, captures revenue between live launches.

Why it's costing Jason: - Cold traffic that arrives May 14 to next-launch goes nowhere - the entire window between live events is unmonetised - Paid traffic can't run profitably to a one-shot live event because the campaign window is too narrow - Warm leads that miss May 13 fall off the email list before the next live date - 30 to 50 percent of typical Year-1 program revenue comes from evergreen replay funnels at this stage

The fix: - Record May 13 webinar at broadcast quality (Riverside or Wistia recording) - Build webinar replay landing page on Squarespace or hosted page that mirrors the live one - 5-day countdown timer starts at opt-in (Deadline Funnel) - 5-touch email sequence over the countdown window, soft-pitching The Flow Code application - Replay page links into the existing Flow Code application form

Estimated impact: Year-1 floor $80,000 to $200,000 in recovered revenue from cold traffic that arrives between live sessions. Compounds with every paid traffic dollar Jason spends. Asymmetric because the webinar he is already running on May 13 becomes the recording.

Why this is #1: Most asymmetric. Lowest install effort. The content is already being filmed. Phase-1 install in less than 7 days from kickoff post-May-13.

Bottleneck #2 - No high-ticket Inner Circle above The Flow Code (INSTALL)

The leak: The top 5 to 8 percent of Flow Code graduates finish the 13-week program with nowhere to go. They have built secure attachment, vision-actualised their next chapter, and want a longer-term room with Jason. Right now there isn't one. No mastermind, no Inner Circle, no quarterly retreat, no extended-cohort tier above the signature program. Graduates either re-enrol (rare) or leave the brand entirely (common).

A $20,000 to $30,000 Inner Circle for graduates - 12 months, small private cohort, monthly group coaching with Jason, two in-person retreats, async voice access for emergent work - converts the top of the existing graduate base into recurring high-ticket revenue.

Why it's costing Jason: - 6 to 10 percent of graduates would buy the next-tier room if it existed - that's the room the brand is missing - Inner Circle members become long-term case studies and named testimonials - the kind that sell every future Flow Code cohort - Without it, the brand caps at the 13-week program and Jason has no recurring high-ticket revenue layer - Most coaches at his stage have an upper-tier room within 18 months of launching their signature program. The Flow Code is past that mark.

The fix: - Inner Circle by application only, graduate-of-Flow-Code prerequisite - $20K-$30K annual investment, 12-month commitment - Monthly group coaching with Jason (90 minutes) - Two in-person retreats per year (3 days each, intentional venues) - Private community channel (Slack or Telegram, not public) - Quarterly 1:1 vision-recalibration call with Jason - Capped at 12 to 20 members for the first cohort

Estimated impact: Year-1 floor 6 to 10 buyers x $25,000 = $150,000 to $250,000. Recurring at renewal, compounding with every Flow Code cohort that graduates into it.

Why this is #2: The brand-completing tier. Highest-ticket layer that's currently empty. Larger Year-1 ceiling than the tripwire but slower install (need 30 to 45 days for offer build + first-cohort fill).

Bottleneck #3 - No tripwire or standalone Self Phase between webinar and program (INSTALL)

The contradiction: Webinar attendees who can't afford the full 13-week Flow Code (or aren't ready for the full commitment) currently exit the funnel entirely. Phase 1 of The Flow Code (the Self phase, weeks 1 to 5: shame work, parts work, somatic work, voice activation) is already built and recorded. Slicing that phase into a $97 to $197 standalone product captures the 5 to 15 percent of webinar attendees who say "I love this but the program isn't right for me yet."

A "Self Phase" mini-course at $97 to $197 - the existing weeks 1 to 5 content, packaged for self-paced delivery - converts non-applicants into buyers and warms them for The Flow Code application 6 to 12 months later.

Why it's costing Jason: - 5 to 15 percent of webinar attendees would buy a $97 to $197 standalone version of Self Phase if it existed - Standalone Self Phase buyers re-enter the funnel as warm-list re-targeting fuel - Some percentage of Self Phase graduates upgrade to the full Flow Code 6 to 12 months later - the tripwire feeds the program - Without it, the entire non-applicant segment of the webinar audience falls off cold

The fix: - Self Phase standalone · 5 weeks of recorded module video pulled from existing Flow Code Self phase - $97 standard tier · self-paced, 6 months access - $197 tier with optional group call (monthly, 60 minutes) - Stripe checkout, automated email delivery, soft pitch to Flow Code application at week 5 - Funnel routing: webinar attendee who declines Flow Code application gets routed to Self Phase landing page automatically

Estimated impact: Year-1 floor $30,000 to $80,000 of pure recovery revenue from webinar attendees who currently exit. Plus the warm-list re-targeting value (Self Phase graduates become Flow Code applicants 6 to 12 months later).

Why this is #3: Smaller ceiling than the Inner Circle, but the easiest pure-recovery layer to install since the content already exists inside the Flow Code Self phase. No new filming, no new methodology, just packaging.

Bottleneck #4 - Distribution-side: audience-yes, X / LinkedIn / YouTube-no (RECURRING ENGAGEMENT)

Snapshot. 102K IG @saintsofflow + 11.5K Threads + active webinar funnel = audience-yes for the brand. But X is essentially absent (no operator handle under the saintsofflow brand), LinkedIn personal profile exists with "I help leaders reach their full potential" headline but no apparent content engine, YouTube long-form home base is underweight for a methodology coach at his tier.

Asymmetry. Jason's stated ICP is LEADERS / EXECUTIVES. LinkedIn is the single highest-intent channel for that ICP. He has the profile, the methodology, the Columbia / Silicon Valley / 8-fig acquisition story, and 102K IG proving his coaching resonates. What's missing is the LinkedIn content engine that takes a CMO scrolling LinkedIn at lunch and routes them to the Flow Code application. AG1's ghostwriting category is operator-tier - fits exactly the leader-to-leader voice Jason already has.

Why it's costing him. Each missing channel has a measurable revenue floor: - X (currently absent): grown to 50K-80K in 12 months drives $20K-$50K/year in Flow Code enrollments - LinkedIn (profile-only): grown to 25K-50K in 12 months drives 4-8 Inner Circle clients at $25K each = $100K-$400K/year - YouTube long-form (underweight): grown to 30K-60K subs feeds the evergreen webinar replay (Bottleneck #1) and drives $30K-$80K/year in Flow Code program enrollments

The fix. Monthly ghostwriting engagement. AG1 writes and ships at Jason's voice from his existing IP (Flow Code 13-week curriculum, Big Three Mastery Workshop transcript, IG reel archive, the Self/Love/Lead methodology footprint). Three channels, one editorial cadence: - X 3x/day (short hooks pulled from Flow Code chapters) - LinkedIn 4-5x/week (leader-positioning essays from the 8-fig acquisition story + methodology) - YouTube long-form 1-2x/week (scripts + thumbnail strategy - Jason or his team films)

Estimated impact. $80K-$300K+ Year-1 floor on the recurring-engagement phase alone. Combined with Install (#1-3): $340K-$830K+ Year-1 total.

Why this is #4 (and the highest-recurring play). Install bottlenecks (#1-3) are one-shot lifts. Distribution is the only layer that compounds - every month the audiences grow and feed the Install layers (YouTube long-form viewers go to evergreen webinar go to Flow Code application). The order matters: Install ships week 1 (evergreen replay + tripwire + Inner Circle scaffold), the recurring engagement kicks in week 2+ as the distribution engine starts capturing demand for the Install-launched product surfaces.
