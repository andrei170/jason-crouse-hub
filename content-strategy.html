Phase 3

Content Strategy - Jason Crouse

Phase 3 deliverable · Hook bank, podcast pivot brief, photoshoot brief, IG / YT short-form roadmap built around the new evergreen replay funnel and Inner Circle layer.

Premise

The @saintsofflow audience is engaged and methodology-curious. The current content cycles through teaching clips, testimonial reposts, and live-launch announcements. None of it routes a viewer into a funnel between live webinar dates. Phase 3 builds a content layer with two routing jobs: send the casual viewer to the evergreen Big Three Mastery replay opt-in, and send the proven graduate to the Inner Circle interest list.

Podcast Pivot Brief

Current state: YouTube @saintsofflow has long-form teaching content but no episodic podcast distribution. LinkedIn carries operator-leaning think pieces but isn't a hosted show.

Phase 3 move: turn the existing YouTube long-form catalog into a 6-episode "Flow Code Field Notes" mini-series. Each episode: 18 to 25 minutes, single-pillar deep-dive (one Self / Love / Lead module per episode + one Inner Circle setup episode + one founder-story origin episode). Pulled from existing material, re-edited with new wraparound commentary. Distribution: YouTube long-form + 6 to 9 short-form cuts per episode for IG reels and YT shorts.

Why: zero new filming pressure on Jason in the immediate post-May-13 window, leverages existing IP, gives the catalog a coherent home, routes viewers to the evergreen Big Three Mastery replay opt-in.

Photoshoot Brief

Two-day shoot, mid-cycle in Phase 3 (Week 7-8 ideal, after the first replay-funnel cohort cycles through).

Day 1 - Outdoor · Earth-Tone Setting: Jason in linen / earth-tone wardrobe at a desert or canyon-adjacent location. Natural light, low-saturation grade matched to the existing brand palette (terracotta, sage, off-white, cream tan). 200+ stills, 3 to 5 hours of B-roll. Reference mood: matte, grounded, somatic, slightly editorial.

Day 2 - Indoor · Methodology Studio Setup: Jason at a desk with notebook, books, tea, plants in frame. Library shelf with the books Jason references publicly. Talking-head B-roll for IG cutdowns. 150+ stills, 2 to 3 hours of talking-head footage. Wardrobe: clean linen or knit, neutral, low-pattern.

Outputs feed: evergreen replay landing page hero, Inner Circle landing page hero, Self Phase standalone landing page, hook bank IG content calendar, ad creative library, Flow Code 2026 cohort pre-sell.

Short-Form Roadmap (90 days)

  • Weeks 4-6: post 5 hooks/week from Categories 1-3 (Self / Love / Lead pain) - the highest-pain trigger segments, route to the evergreen replay opt-in
  • Weeks 7-9: post 5/week from Categories 4-6 (Big Three Bridge / Methodology + Story / Graduate Stories) - build mid-funnel trust and route to Self Phase tripwire
  • Weeks 10-12: post 5/week from Categories 7-8 (Inner Circle / Replay + Live Webinar) + retargeting ads layered on top of organic

Cadence: 5 IG reels + 5 YT shorts + 1 long-form / week. Repurpose ratio 1:5 - one filmed asset = one long-form episode + 5 short cuts.

Conversion Goal

Every piece of content has the same final CTA: "The Big Three Mastery Workshop replay - free, on-demand, 5-day countdown to apply for The Flow Code." Link in bio routes through Linktree to the evergreen replay opt-in. Inner Circle routing happens via a separate audience segment (Flow Code graduates only).
