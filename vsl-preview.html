Phase 1 deliverable · 3-5 minute preview script for the Big Three Mastery evergreen replay landing page hero. Short preview only - the full evergreen replay is the recorded May 13 live workshop.

Format

3 to 5 minute preview VSL hosted on Wistia, embedded above the fold on the evergreen replay opt-in landing page. The preview's only job is to get the visitor to opt in for the full Big Three Mastery Workshop replay. Short, restrained, methodology-first.

Cold Open (00:00 - 00:30)

On camera, Jason:

"If you've been working on yourself for a while and the outer change still hasn't shown up, it's almost never because you didn't try hard enough. It's because the order is off. Career, love, confidence are one arc, not three problems. The Big Three Mastery Workshop walks the order. This is the recorded version. The full session is on the next page if you want it."

The Frame (00:30 - 01:30)

The three lanes most people treat as separate: - Career - the one you keep restarting - Love - the partnership that keeps repeating - Confidence - the voice that keeps going quiet at the moment that matters

The thread between them is internal. The order is Self, then Love, then Lead. Most people skip Self and wonder why Love and Lead never integrate. The workshop walks why.

Who I Am (01:30 - 02:30)

Quick credentials, no chronology theatre: - 15 years in personal development - Homeless and jobless to Ivy League graduate to Silicon Valley operator - Founding-team member on a high 8-figure acquisition - Founder of Saints of Flow and creator of The Flow Code - The methodology was built from the inside out, not from a textbook

The point isn't the resume. The point is the work behind it.

What the Full Workshop Covers (02:30 - 03:30)

The recorded Big Three Mastery Workshop walks: - The Big Three framework (career, love, confidence as one arc) - The Self phase practices that have to come first - The Love phase work that resolves the anxious-avoidant loop - The Lead phase architecture for vision that actually actualises - The 90-day arc inside The Flow Code, for graduates of the workshop who want the full integration

Not theory. Practice-first.

Who This Is For (03:30 - 04:00)

This is for: people who have read the books, done the courses, sat with the journals, and still feel the gap between insight and integration. People who want methodology, not motivation.

This is not for: anyone looking for a quick fix or a manifestation script. The work is real. The arc is 90 days inside the program. The workshop is the on-ramp.

Close (04:00 - 04:30)

The full workshop is on the next page. Opt in below. After the replay, you'll have 5 days to apply for the next Flow Code cohort if it's a fit.

"Watch the full session. See what lands. The button's below. - Jason."

Production Notes

  • Filming location: Same earth-tone setting as Jason's existing brand surfaces. Outdoor or window-light interior. Linen / neutral wardrobe.
  • Camera: main camera + mic'd. Sony FX3 or equivalent + Rode Wireless Pro. B-camera optional for cutaways.
  • Wardrobe: clean linen or knit, low-pattern, earth-tone palette.
  • Lighting: natural where possible; soft key + fill if interior.
  • Pace: slow but not sleepy. No filler. B-roll cuts on key transitions only (not every 15 seconds - this is methodology video, not commercial video).
  • Music: none under talking head. Ambient bed under cold open + close only. Royalty-free, library-grade, low-frequency.
  • Captions: burnt-in for IG / YT shorts; SRT for long-form Wistia.
  • Thumbnail (Wistia poster): Jason looking at camera, grounded posture, earth-tone background. Title overlay: "The order matters."

Short Cuts (for paid traffic + IG)

From the preview, edit: - 3 x 60-90 sec hook-led trailers (one per phase: Self / Love / Lead) - 6 x 30-45 sec methodology cuts (one per workshop section) - 6 x 15-20 sec IG reel hooks pulled from cold open and frame sections

Distribution
